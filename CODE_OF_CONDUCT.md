# Working together

Be respectful, specific and curious. Discuss behavior, evidence and tradeoffs rather than judging a contributor. Explain requested changes and distinguish a defect from a preference. Questions and disagreement are welcome; harassment and personal attacks are not.

Prefer small reviewable changes, reproducible bug reports and clear explanations. Do not include credentials or private app/user data in reports. Maintainers may moderate harmful interactions and are not promising response times.

Report sensitive security issues privately to the maintainer rather than in a public issue.
